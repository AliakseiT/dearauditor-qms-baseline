# records/ai_act - AI Act provider compliance records

Packaged record templates for the regulatory artifacts required by
`sops/SOP-024-AIActProviderQMS.md`. Companion to `records/aims/` (AIMS
organisational management system, ISO/IEC 42001) and `records/mdf/`
(medical device technical documentation). The AI system *inventory* stays in
the AIMS register to avoid two lists that drift.

| Template | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `regulatory_compliance_strategy_template.md` | Written strategy per EU AI Act Art. 17(1)(a), incl. conformity assessment route and post-market monitoring plan. |
| `technical_specification_selection_register_template.yml` | Register of measures selected to demonstrate conformity, with justification and gaps (Art. 17(1)(e)). |
| `ai_system_requirements_spec_template.yml` | AI system requirements mapped to Art. 9-15 essential requirements. |
| `fundamental_rights_consultation_template.md` | Structured consultation with affected persons and interested parties. |
| `ai_data_governance_record_template.yml` | Dataset provenance, origin, licensing, representativeness, bias examination, handling (clause 8.5). |
| `environmental_sustainability_assessment_template.md` | Environmental impact identification and mitigation (SOP-024 §6.6). |
| `pre_determined_change_plan_template.md` | Substantial vs non-substantial determination and pre-determined change plans (clauses 8.8, 9.4). |
| `serious_incident_report_template.md` | AI Act serious incident report with 2/10/15-day clock and provisional-final cycle. |
| `authority_cooperation_log_template.md` | Inbound reasoned requests and responses (clause 7.3). |
